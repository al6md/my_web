About the Book

"Elham" (Arabic for Inspiration) is more than just a collection of essays; it is a meticulously structured roadmap for the creative mind. Dr. Kareem Al-Hassan bridges the gap between traditional Eastern philosophy and modern psychological insights, offering a unique perspective on how we perceive and capture "the spark."

The book is divided into four cardinal directions: The Silence of Observation, The Weight of Craft, The Dialogue with History, and The Architecture of Legacy. Each chapter challenges the reader to look beyond the surface of their everyday life and find the sacred in the mundane.

"Inspiration is not a lightning bolt that strikes the idle; it is a persistent sun that rises for those who have spent the night preparing their fields."

With over 400 pages of profound wisdom, exquisite illustrations, and practical exercises, Elham is designed to be a lifelong companion for poets, designers, leaders, and anyone who believes that to live is to create.
