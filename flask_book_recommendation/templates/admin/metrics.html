Metrics Explanation
Click-Through Rate (CTR)

The percentage of recommended books that users actually clicked on or interacted with.

Precision@K

Measures how many of the top K recommendations were actually relevant to the user.

Recall@K

Measures how many of the user's total true preferences were captured in the top K recommendations.

NDCG

Measures the ranking quality. A perfect ranking (most relevant items first) scores 1.0.

Diversity Score

Measures the breadth of topics shown to the user to prevent echo-chambers and encourage discovery.
